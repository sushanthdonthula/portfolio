About the ISM Program

Frisco ISD’s Independent Study and Mentorship (ISM) program is a rigorous course for which high-achieving students apply, interview, and earn recommendations during their junior or senior year. Students focus their study on a self-selected career topic, conducting professional interviews and developing an extensive research portfolio.


Throughout the course, students refine time management, public speaking, research, and soft skills. During the spring semester, students collaborate with a professional mentor in their field of interest to gain real-world experience and build an advanced final product presented in May.
